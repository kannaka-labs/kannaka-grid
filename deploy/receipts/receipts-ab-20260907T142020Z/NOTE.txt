TWO THINGS FOR NICK, BOTH RAISED ON THE COORDINATION BOARD

The `current` alias was re-pointed onto the 7b adapter by hand, and the relay
moved with it on 09-06 with no committed receipt. The gate promotes a winner by
copying it onto that alias, so a by-hand promotion leaves no ledger row: the
gate still reports released=null and would flip the alias out from under that
choice on the first Sunday a candidate wins.

The abort's arms snapshot covers all recorded history, not an A/B window, so it
is a hint and not a result — but it is a loud one. Over that history the 14b v3
reproduced 8 of 33 settled proposals (0.24) against the 7b's 1 of 49 (0.02).
If that gap survives a real A/B, the 7b promotion cost an order of magnitude of
proposal quality.
